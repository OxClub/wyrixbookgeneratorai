Privacy Policy

App: WYRIX - AI Book Generator & Story Writer (package com.wyrix.bookgenerator)
Last updated: November 2026
Contact: support@yourdomain.com

1. Summary

WYRIX does not run any server and does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Everything you create stays on your device, except the text that is sent to the AI providers you choose to connect (see section 3).

2. Data stored on your device

  • Books and prompts you create are stored in a local database on your phone. You can delete any book inside the app, or clear the app's storage, to remove them.
  • API keys you enter are stored encrypted using the Android Keystore. They are never sent to us.
  • Exported files (PDF/EPUB) are saved to your Downloads folder when you tap export.

3. Data sent to third-party AI services

To write your book, the app sends your prompt and the book text generated so far directly from your device to the AI service(s) for which you entered an API key (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral, SambaNova, NVIDIA NIM, GitHub Models, Hugging Face, or a custom provider you configure). These requests use your own API key and are governed by that provider's privacy policy and terms. We do not receive a copy of this data.

4. Permissions

  • Internet - to contact the AI services.
  • Notifications - to show book generation progress.
  • Foreground service (data sync) - to keep long book generation running when the app is in the background.

5. Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. AI-generated content

Content is produced by third-party AI models and may be inaccurate or unsuitable. Please review it before sharing or publishing. You can report offensive content from inside the app (Reader or About screen) or by email.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export. All data can be removed by deleting books in the app or uninstalling it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
