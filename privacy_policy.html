1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Android application "WYRIX - AI Book Generator & Story Writer" (the "App") handles, why, where it goes, and what choices you have. It applies to the App as downloaded from Google Play or installed from the project's official GitHub releases.

The App lets you write a prompt describing a book or story, choose a type, language and approximate length, and then uses artificial intelligence services to write the book in the background. You can read the result inside the App and export it as PDF or EPUB.

By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Definitions

  • "We", "us", "Developer" means OxClub, the publisher of the App.
  • "You" means the person using the App.
  • "Device" means the Android phone or tablet on which the App is installed.
  • "AI Provider" means a third-party service that runs an AI model and which you connect to the App by entering your own API key (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral, SambaNova, NVIDIA NIM, GitHub Models, Hugging Face, or a custom OpenAI-compatible service).
  • "API key" means the secret token issued to you by an AI Provider.
  • "Content" means the prompts you type and the books, chapters and text the AI writes for you.
  • "Personal data" means information that identifies, or can reasonably be linked to, a specific person.

3. Who we are

The App is developed and published by OxClub. We are an independent developer. We do not operate a backend server, a user database, a login system or a payment system for this App. Because of this design we do not hold personal data about you.

5. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of the following through the App:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address;

The App does not ask for any permission to access these categories.

6. Information stored on your device

6.1 Local database

Each book you create is saved in a private database inside the App's storage area on your Device. It contains your prompt, the chosen type (genre), language and page target, the planned title and outline, a running summary used to keep the story consistent, and every section of text the AI has written. This database is accessible only to the App (and to you through the App) under normal Android security rules.

6.2 Preferences

The App keeps small settings such as your API keys (see section 7) and a cached list of AI model names fetched from each provider, so it does not need to request the list every time. The model list contains no personal data.

6.3 Exported files

When you tap PDF or EPUB, the App saves a file into your Device's Downloads/WyrixBookGenerator folder. These files are yours; the App does not upload them anywhere. Other apps with access to your Downloads folder may be able to see them, as with any file you save there.

6.4 Android backup

Android's automatic backup feature may copy the App's local database to your own Google account if you have device backup turned on. This is controlled by your Device settings and your Google account, not by us. We have configured the App so that API keys are excluded from backup and device-to-device transfer.

7. Your API keys

To use the App you enter one or more API keys. We designed the App so that:

  • keys are stored using Android's encrypted preferences, protected by a key held in the Android Keystore on your Device;
  • keys are used only to authenticate requests that go directly from your Device to the matching AI Provider;
  • keys are never shown in plain text in the App's key fields (they are masked) and are never sent to the Developer or to any server we control;
  • keys are excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

You are responsible for keeping your keys secret. If you believe a key has leaked, delete it in your provider's dashboard and create a new one. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes the stored keys from your Device.

Instructions for obtaining a key from each provider are shown inside the App under each key field.

8. Data sent to AI providers

8.1 What is sent

To write your book the App sends requests over the internet (HTTPS) to the AI Providers you connected. A request can contain: your prompt; the selected genre, language and length; the book plan and a short running summary; the end of the previously written text for continuity; and, in "Both languages" mode, the text to be translated. It also contains your API key in the request header so the provider can authenticate you.

8.3 How they use it

Each AI Provider processes your request under its own terms and privacy policy. Some providers, especially on free tiers, may log requests or use them to improve their products. We do not control this and we receive no copy of your data. Please read the privacy policy of each provider whose key you add, and do not put confidential, sensitive or personal information into prompts if you are not comfortable with that provider handling it.

8.4 Automatic fallback

If one provider or model is busy or out of free quota, the App automatically tries another model from the providers you connected. This means different parts of the same book may be processed by different providers.

10. Background generation and notifications

When you start a book, the App schedules a background job using Android WorkManager. While the job runs, a persistent notification shows progress (for example the current section). If you pause, the job is cancelled. If the internet connection drops or a free quota runs out, the job pauses and retries automatically. Notifications are generated locally; no push-notification server is used.

11. Exporting and sharing files

After export, the App can open the file in another app on your Device or hand it to the Android share sheet. When you share a file, it is sent to the app or person you choose, and from that point their privacy practices apply. The App itself does not transmit the file.

12. Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS (clear-text traffic is disabled in the App).
  • API keys are stored encrypted using Android Keystore-backed encryption.
  • The App's database lives in private app storage.
  • The release build is code-shrunk and signed with the Developer's key.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and a rooted or compromised Device may weaken these protections. Keep your Device updated and protected with a screen lock.

13. Content safety and prohibited content

WYRIX is intended for creating lawful, general-audience books and stories. The App does not allow, and actively blocks, requests for:

  • sexually explicit, erotic or pornographic content, or nudity;
  • any sexual content involving minors, or content that sexualises or endangers children;
  • sexual violence;
  • hate speech, harassment or content that promotes violence against people or groups;
  • instructions for weapons, explosives, crimes or other illegal activity;
  • content that encourages suicide or self-harm.

13.1 How blocking works

The App applies several layers: (1) a filter checks your prompt in English, Hinglish and Hindi before generation starts; (2) every request to an AI model carries safety rules that instruct the model to refuse such content; (3) the generated text is scanned for explicit language and, if found, is rewritten once and otherwise generation is stopped and the book is marked "BLOCKED". These checks run on your Device. No content is sent to us for moderation.

13.2 Limits

Automated filters are not perfect. They may occasionally block harmless requests or miss unsuitable text. Please review all generated content before using or sharing it.

13.3 Reporting

If the App produces content that is offensive, unsafe or unlawful, use the "Report content" button in the Reader or About screen, or contact us using the details in section 24. Please include a short description (do not send your API keys). We will review reports and improve the filters.

You are responsible for your prompts and for how you use the output. Using the App to create content that violates the law or the policies of Google Play or your AI Provider is not permitted.

14. AI-generated content and ownership

Books are written by third-party AI models. They may contain factual mistakes, outdated or biased statements, invented names or events, repeated passages, or text that resembles existing works. AI-generated text should not be relied on as professional medical, legal, financial or other expert advice.

As between you and us, we claim no ownership over the Content you create with the App. Whether and how AI-generated text can be protected by copyright, and who owns it, depends on your country's law and on the terms of the AI Provider you used. Please check those terms before publishing or selling a book.

15. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is not designed for a child audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because the App can generate free-form text through third-party AI services, we recommend that children use it only with a parent or guardian. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the App in a way you are not comfortable with, you can remove all App data by uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, we have nothing to access, correct, delete or transfer on our side. You can exercise control over your data directly: delete books in the App, clear its storage, uninstall it, remove your API keys, or use the privacy controls of the AI Providers you connected.

You may contact us at any time with a privacy question (section 24). If you believe your rights have been violated you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

18. International data transfers

AI Providers operate servers in various countries. When your prompts are sent to them, the data may be processed outside the country where you live, under that provider's terms. We do not receive or transfer your data ourselves.
